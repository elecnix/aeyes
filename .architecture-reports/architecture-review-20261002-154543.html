Top recommendation

Consolidate pixel-format knowledge into one module — then hang the converters off it

It wins on every axis at once, and the history is unusually clear about why.

7
sites must change to add one pixel format today. Commits #27 and #29 both paid that tax.
5 of 7
formats silently skip exposure adaptation at lib.rs:731 — the exact formats #29 just added.
20 lines
of luma analysis duplicated verbatim, over a shared histogram that was never extracted.

Three independent findings — the converter family, the scattered fourcc list, and the exposure dispatch — collapse into one seam. That is the signature of a genuinely deep module rather than a tidy-up: the interface gets smaller (callers stop naming fourccs) while it hides more (plane geometry, chroma addressing, the encoder choice). It also unblocks the exposure work, which is currently blocked precisely because it needs a format module to ask.
